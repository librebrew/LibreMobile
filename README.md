# The LibreMobile

This is a project I've wanted to attempt for a little while due to the absurdly high gasoline prices within the USA.
Under Georgia law, the finished build will technically be classified as an "electrically-assisted bicycle" and requires no permit or license to operate (unless you modify the design).
This project will comply with the conditions and restrictions marked below by Georgia state law:

- Saddle and working pedals
- Motor of 750W or less
- Bicycle equipment and a motor cutout
- Motor assist speed limited to 20 mph (32.1869 km/h) or less.

Attempt this at your own risk. I am no qualified engineer and I am not responsible for any damages or injuries sustained by my design, nor can I be held accountable for failures on your or my end.

Most, if not all of this project is hand made, with the exception being anything marked independently as "AI assisted", such as the color-coded diagram of the individual chassis components.

This frame is untested. I don't know what you're supposed to do with this information, but just in case you try to sue me, this frame is 100% untested against ANY real world conditions, including but not limited to:

- Fires
- Impacts (by vehicle or by object)
- Rollovers
- Roadside failures (such as a structural failure)

You are expected to figure out what fasteners to use and how to fasten this chassis, if you're making this out of wood (like me).

Weather sealing is recommended if you are considering driving this "apparatus" in any condition other than hot-as-balls.

Please for the love of god wear basic protective gear when "driving" this thing. You people cannot be trusted with a plastic spork, I do not trust you to make sure that this chassis is held together correctly.
